Language Bear pilot

Put your language into the bear.

Record or upload your approved recordings for each of the bear's seven touch points. When you are done, this page makes a package that Indigenize Toys loads into the bears.

How it reaches us. This page does not upload anything. It builds one .zip package and saves it to your computer. Email that file to jon@indigenize.toys as an attachment, or share it through Google Drive if it is too large for email.
